ABOUT
THE LAB

Coaching built around the person doing the work.

APEX Training Lab is a gym and fitness centre in Heraklion built around one idea: training works best when it's built for the person doing it.

No two members need the same approach. Every session is designed around your goal, your starting point and your progress.

Whether you train one-on-one or in a mini group, the focus stays on quality coaching, correct technique and purposeful training.
