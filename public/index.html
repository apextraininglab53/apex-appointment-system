About
the Lab

Coaching built around the person doing the work.

APEX Training Lab is a gym and fitness centre in Heraklion built around one idea: training works best when it's built for the person doing it. No two members need the same approach — each plan is shaped around your own goal, your own starting point and your own schedule.

Every session is coached, not just supervised. Whether you train in a mini group or one-on-one, the focus stays on quality — correct technique, sensible loading, and sessions that move you toward your maximum potential.
